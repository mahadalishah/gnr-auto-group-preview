ABOUT US

WHO WE ARE

At GNR, we believe business mobility and vehicle ownership should be simple, secure, and stress-free. With years of experience in leasing, insurance, and business solutions, we’ve built a reputation for being a reliable partner to both drivers and companies.
Our mission is straightforward: make it easier for people and businesses to move forward. Whether it’s through flexible leasing plans, tailored insurance coverage, or practical business solutions, everything we do is designed around your needs.

At GNR Auto Leasing, we believe success should be shared. That’s why we donate 10% of our monthly profits to a charity of our choice, supporting causes that make a real difference in people’s lives. Our goal goes beyond providing affordable, flexible vehicle leasing, we’re also committed to creating a positive social impact. Every time you lease with GNR, you’re not just getting on the road, you’re helping drive change for a better world.
